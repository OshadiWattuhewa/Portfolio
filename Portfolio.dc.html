Case study
Personal project · Mobile

Travel mate

A human-centered approach to travel planning

Planning a trip should be exciting — but for many travellers it quickly becomes overwhelming. Instead of looking forward to the journey, people find themselves switching between countless websites, social posts, blogs, review platforms, maps and booking apps just to answer simple questions:

  • What are the must-visit places?
  • When is the best time to visit?
  • Where do locals actually eat?
  • Which transport option is the most convenient?
  • What do real travellers recommend — and what should I avoid?

While AI can generate itineraries in seconds, it often misses the context that makes travel memorable. It can't always capture authentic local experiences, changing seasonal conditions, hidden gems, or the lessons learned by people who have actually been there.

TravelMate was born from this insight.

02 — Pain points & goals

What Travelers Struggle With

Understanding the real challenges travelers face throughout their journey — from discovering destinations and planning itineraries to managing budgets and making confident decisions.

Pain points
  • Planning takes too long, with no clear starting point — what order to visit places in, the best route, how to build day plans.
  • Travel posts and photo locations end up saved across different social platforms with no single place to keep them.
  • No confidence about where to gather useful, accurate information.
  • After each trip it feels like more research would have made some stops better.
  • Transport options between places — and realistic time per visit — are stressful to work out.
  • Getting a feel for one place often means sitting through long videos. It's slow and boring.
  • Social media shows exaggerated images, so expectations and reality don't match.
  • Constantly hunting for budget-friendly travel options.
  • Searching Google and social media for affordable shopping — clothing, groceries, shoes.
  • Hard to track the expected budget as new items get added to the itinerary.
  • Transport apps differ country to country, so every trip starts with research on which to use.
  • Always having to ask friends or neighbours who travelled there before.
  • Limited awareness of available data and internet options.
  • Separate apps for accommodation, transport and things to do — syncing them is frustrating.
Goals
  • One easy, time-saving platform to plan trips and explore the details.
  • A friendly way to find things to do, good restaurants, shopping places and adventures.
  • Sensible route suggestions for each trip.
  • One place to save all posts, blogs and videos.
  • A shared space to connect with other travellers for trustworthy tips — and to build a travel community.
  • Reviews and content grounded in real user experience.
  • An easy way to handle the total trip budget.
  • Surface the most budget-friendly options to travel.
  • Customised itineraries based on each user's needs and goals.
  • Travel bucket lists and saved inspirational posts.
  • Post travel experiences and share them with the community.
03 — Solution blueprint

The shape of the answer.

Based on the challenges identified, I mapped out a solution framework focused on simplifying the travel planning journey. The blueprint defines the key experiences, features, and interactions needed to help travelers discover, organize, and plan their trips with more confidence and less effort.
