How it works
  • Users can search for a specific
    • Country (Malaysia)
    • City (Kuala Lumpur)
    • Place (KL Tower)
  • Country
    • Things to do: nature, adventure, culture, sports
    • Main travel destinations: what each city is famous for and its top attractions
    • Best time to travel
    • Food and restaurants
    • Shopping, transport, accommodation
    • Internet and data packages
  • City
    • Things to do: nature, adventure, culture, shopping
    • Food and restaurants
    • Opening times, transport, accommodation
  • Place
    • Good to know: city, waiting time, tickets, average cost per head, best time to visit, distance from the nearest main city, transport
    • Reviews
    • Photos and videos
  • Opening a specific item shows
    • Good to know points
    • Photos and videos to like, share, comment on and save for later
    • Reviews, where users can read and add their own
  • Things to do are grouped into nature, adventure, culture and sports.
  • Opening an item shows
    • Photos and videos
    • Reviews
    • Good to know: city, waiting time, tickets and where to buy them, average cost per head, best time to visit by season, distance from the nearest main city
  • Things are grouped by main cities.
  • Each city has a summary of what it is famous for: nature, adventure, culture or shopping.
  • Users can easily pick the cities they want to visit.
  • Opening a city shows the same things to do list as above.
  • Key points travellers care about for a country
    • Best time to travel
    • Transport
    • Best way to book accommodation
    • Authentic food to try
    • Data packages
    • Money exchange places
  • Tap "Add to trip" to add items to a trip.
  • Creating a new trip needs sign up or sign in. Otherwise users are sent to the sign in screen when they tap "Add to trip".
  • Items can go into an existing trip or a new one.
Technical delightsSuggest matching items while typing. The information architecture changes for a country, city or place.Reorder categories based on interests gathered at onboarding.
  • List items from most to least popular.
  • Suggest items based on onboarding preferences.

  • Opening an item reuses the explore feed component.
  • Use accordions to present each section.
