Case study
Personal project · Mobile

Travel mate

A human-centered approach to travel planning

Planning a trip should be exciting — but for many travellers it quickly becomes overwhelming. Instead of looking forward to the journey, people find themselves switching between countless websites, social posts, blogs, review platforms, maps and booking apps just to answer simple questions:

  • What are the must-visit places?
  • When is the best time to visit?
  • Where do locals actually eat?
  • Which transport option is the most convenient?
  • What do real travellers recommend — and what should I avoid?

While AI can generate itineraries in seconds, it often misses the context that makes travel memorable. It can't always capture authentic local experiences, changing seasonal conditions, hidden gems, or the lessons learned by people who have actually been there.

TravelMate was born from this insight.

02 — Pain points & goals

What Travelers Struggle With

Understanding the real challenges travelers face throughout their journey from discovering destinations and planning itineraries to managing budgets and making confident decisions.

Pain points14 frustrations from research
  • 01Planning takes too long, with no clear starting point: what order to visit places in, the best route, how to build day plans.
  • 02Travel posts and photo locations end up saved across different social platforms with no single place to keep them.
  • 03No confidence about where to gather useful, accurate information.
  • 04After each trip it feels like more research would have made some stops better.
  • 05Transport options between places, and realistic time per visit, are stressful to work out.
  • 06Getting a feel for one place often means sitting through long videos. It's slow and boring.
  • 07Social media shows exaggerated images, so expectations and reality don't match.
  • 08Constantly hunting for budget friendly travel options.
  • 09Searching Google and social media for affordable shopping, like clothing, groceries and shoes.
  • 10Hard to track the expected budget as new items get added to the itinerary.
  • 11Transport apps differ country to country, so every trip starts with research on which to use.
  • 12Always having to ask friends or neighbours who travelled there before.
  • 13Limited awareness of available data and internet options.
  • 14Separate apps for accommodation, transport and things to do, and syncing them is frustrating.
Goals11 things travellers want
  • 01One easy, time-saving platform to plan trips and explore the details.
  • 02A friendly way to find things to do, good restaurants, shopping places and adventures.
  • 03Sensible route suggestions for each trip.
  • 04One place to save all posts, blogs and videos.
  • 05A shared space to connect with other travellers for trustworthy tips and to build a travel community.
  • 06Reviews and content grounded in real user experience.
  • 07An easy way to handle the total trip budget.
  • 08Surface the most budget friendly options to travel.
  • 09Customised itineraries based on each user's needs and goals.
  • 10Travel bucket lists and saved inspirational posts.
  • 11Post travel experiences and share them with the community.

Solution overview

01
User onboarding & initial setup
  • App download & onboarding — the user downloads the app and enters an onboarding flow where they select interest topics (e.g., nature, food, culture).
  • Preference engine — user preferences are captured to power personalized recommendations across the community feed and trip suggestions.
02
Authentication
  • Users can proceed through standard authentication (Login / Signup) or bypass detailed entry via a streamlined flow (Skip Login / Signup).
03
Core features & engagement
  • Create new trips & alarms — set up travel timelines, schedules, and custom reminders/alarms.
  • Search / plan trip — search destination details by country, city, or specific places.
  • Post travel content — share travel pictures, videos, or write blog posts.
  • Community interaction — connect with other travelers, follow user profiles, chat, and interact with community content.
  • Community feed — browse posts and travel experiences shared by other users.
  • AI travel agent — conversational AI capabilities to assist users in planning trips interactively.
04
Itinerary creation & budgeting
  • Save items — save places, activities, and ideas to a specific trip itinerary.
  • Smart recommendations — the system processes saved items to generate dynamic trip/itinerary suggestions.
  • Check suggested itinerary — users can review and refine system-recommended itineraries tailored to their saved preferences.
  • Set up budget — define and manage travel budgets directly within the planned trip.
